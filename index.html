Why Açaí?

The Amazon's superfood, one bowl at a time.

Açaí (ah-sigh-EE) berries grow wild on palm trees deep in the Amazon rainforest, where Brazilian communities have eaten them for generations. We source ours from sustainable growers and blend it fresh — no added sugar needed, the berry does the work.

Antioxidant Powerhouse

Açaí is packed with anthocyanins — the same antioxidants that give red wine its reputation, but even more concentrated.

Heart-Healthy Fats

Rich in monounsaturated fats, similar to olive oil, that support healthy cholesterol levels.

Fiber & Digestion

A single bowl delivers a solid dose of dietary fiber to keep you full and your gut happy.

Natural Energy

Complex carbs and healthy fats give you steady energy without the crash of refined sugar.

Vitamin-Rich

Loaded with vitamin A, calcium, and iron sourced straight from the rainforest.

Anti-Inflammatory

Compounds in açaí are linked to reduced inflammation — a favorite among athletes and yogis alike.
